# Data handling

What Stonewake ingests, stores, and serves, on one page. The research base is registers, courts, regulators, tenders, filings and the trade press; your workspace data stays yours.

## Sources and citations

- The research base is public data only. It covers official company registers and gazettes, court and insolvency publications, regulatory filings, the LEI system, public procurement records, official statistics, licensed reference datasets, and reputable press.
- Findings carry citations to their public origin, down to the quoted passage, so every claim can be checked at its source.
- Scores apply to organizations and countries, never to natural persons. A sole trader's business counts as a natural person, by the registers' own evidence (a German legal form of a natural person, the e.K. or e.U. designation of the firm name, or a sole proprietor's legal form or category in the GLEIF record). Where these sources disagree, the German register extract decides when there is one; without it, any of them stating a natural person's business decides, and the disagreement is recorded for review. It carries no score, status, status change, risk alert, news tone or social sentiment, and no such value of one is kept, while its register facts, insolvency notices, news articles, news events and social posts are read like a company's. A person your workspace watches carries the tone of its news and posts as context for your institution, never as a score of the person.
- Where person data is switched on for a workspace, the natural persons that official registers and the official sanctions lists name (officers, shareholders, beneficial owners and listed persons) are served to that workspace with their citations, in the dashboard, the API and MCP alike, for know your customer, sanctions and credit due diligence on organisations and their people. The sandbox and our public pages serve none. A person who objects is no longer shown as a register or list person on any of these surfaces, and a person who leaves a sanctions list is deleted from our copy of it in the same update. Adverse media screenings of natural persons, which your analysts request in the dashboard, and research runs about a natural person are read through the API and MCP under the same switch, as findings with their sources and never with a verdict or a score. Such a screening holds a name and no register entry, so an objection is answered by erasing it, together with any dossier on the person. A natural person your workspace watches is followed in news and social monitoring and alerts like a company, and a person can be a research target with the purpose your analyst records, whose runs are erased with the target. None of them is ever scored. The [public records notice](https://stonewake.ai/public-records-notice) names the sources and the recipients. Every API and MCP response that serves a natural person writes one access record naming the workspace, the API key, the route and the ids of the persons, records, screenings or runs it served, never a name. This covers a risk row that names a listed person.

## Retention

- Workspace data is retained while your arrangement is active. Bank-instructed screenings are kept for the retention horizon agreed with your bank, or until erased where none is agreed, and every screening and every dossier on a person is erasable on demand.
- The database is backed up every night to separate storage in Finland, and each backup is encrypted before it leaves the server.
- On offboarding you export your reports and data during a wind-down period; workspace data is then deleted, and the entries of the audit trail are retained after the arrangement ends. They hold identifiers, among them the email address of a member an administrator invited or removed or who first signed in through single sign on, and no screening content, except feedback reports, which keep their text and the reporter's email address until both are removed from entries older than 180 days. The [API security](/security) page carries the same posture for the programmatic surface.

## Legal basis and processors

- The GDPR basis for each processing purpose is set out in the [privacy policy](https://stonewake.ai/privacy); public-register facts follow the [public records notice](https://stonewake.ai/public-records-notice).
- The database, the API, the audit trail, the MCP server, the dashboard, the documentation and the website run on a dedicated server in Finland. A provider established in the United States delivers the web pages of the dashboard, the documentation and the website over its global delivery network. Sign in runs through an authentication service provider that keeps the sign in accounts in the United Kingdom. Search queries, fetched public text and the text sent to AI providers for drafting, including the subject of a screening, are processed by search and AI providers as described in the privacy policy, sections 7 and 8. For a workspace that its contract places on the EU route, every language model call is processed in the European Union, and a call that cannot be served there fails instead of going to another provider. A workspace can be limited by arrangement to a named set of these search providers and to a text embedding model of its own. The full processor list and the Article 32 measures are part of the data processing agreement provided to customer banks.
- On the standard route, which serves every workspace that its contract does not place on the EU route, our language model provider stores prompts in the United States and may process them in the United States, Europe, Asia or Australia, and our text embedding provider processes text in the United States. Where that language model provider cannot be used for a call, the call goes to a fallback provider in a country for which the European Commission has not issued an adequacy decision, and where that one cannot be used either, to a second fallback provider in another such country, except a call about a natural person, which never goes to the second. The provider of the sign in accounts is established in a country for which the European Commission has not issued an adequacy decision, and transfers to it rest on the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) that its data processing terms incorporate.
- Sources your bank brings, the alerts of its own tender portal accounts and the values of a company data provider it licenses, are processed for your bank only and stay in its workspace. Such a provider is called under your bank's own key, on your bank's instruction, and the key is stored encrypted for your bank only. See [Your own sources](/own-sources).
- We store your workspace records on our infrastructure and do not train models on them.
- Stonewake trains no model on your data, and the terms of our language model provider bar it from training models on customer content. Every other provider that receives text from us, for text embeddings, as a fallback or for search, handles it under its own terms, and the AI controls page shows your administrators what the published terms of each provider say about training.
- Your bank and an auditor it mandates may audit our processing, inspections included, under clause 10 of the data processing agreement, and for a bank under DORA its clause 12.1 extends that right to the bank's competent authorities and resolution authorities.
- The audit trail records, for each research run, screening, screening summary, deal briefing run, report and pack that called a language model, the provider and model that answered each of its calls, a fallback provider included, and the model that was planned where it differs. A record that cannot show which model answered says so and never names the planned model instead. On the AI controls page an administrator sees these calls counted per provider and model for the last 30 days.
