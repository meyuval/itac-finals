# EDD dossiers

The EDD dossier workbench runs enhanced due diligence on a company or a person in stages, end to end on its own, and every check cites its evidence. Register identity, global screening and the company's footprint come first, then the register checks of each country the company is present in.

## Where to find it

- **EDD Dossiers** in the sidebar opens the workbench for every team whose workspace holds the dossiers module.
- **Start EDD dossier** on a company's page opens the workbench with a request preset to that company.
- **New dossier** on the workbench requests one for any company or person.

## Requesting a dossier

An analyst or above requests a dossier and states its purpose, which a dossier on a person always needs. Each stage is recorded as it completes. Where the footprint the dossier proposes is empty, the dossier waits for you to confirm it, and **Retry** runs a failed stage again. **Download report** on a dossier's page produces it as a PDF. Viewers read their team's dossiers and request none, and compliance members read the dossiers of every team.

## Where it is served

The workbench is part of the dashboard, and the audit log records each dossier by its id. A company's register identity, screenings and register records that a dossier reads are also served over the [API](/v1) and to AI assistants over [MCP](/mcp).
