Each list reads `listed` (only on an identifier that the list itself states and the subject's own register entry carries), `name_match_awaiting_review` (a name without an identifier, a candidate for review and never a hit, shown with the list entry and the subject side by side), `name_match_reviewed`, `no_entry_found` (with the list edition and the check time) or `not_checked` (with the reason). Names match exactly on a normalised key, so other spellings, word orders and transliterations are not found. A list that is switched off or was last checked more than 48 hours ago never reads as clear, so every list reads `not_checked` until it is switched on. The screening report PDF carries the section first with the list names, editions, check times and the moment of the comparison. Once at least one list was compared it is titled Due diligence screening, and its cover states a listing, an open name match and how many official sanctions lists were compared. The Excel export carries the same in its new Official lists sheet, placed before the Screenings sheet. No date of birth and no nationality of a listed person is served on any surface. The MCP tool `screening_lookup` forwards the section. The section does not replace your own sanctions and PEP screening. See the [API reference](/v1).
- 30 September. The screening report PDF states on its cover how many of its findings carry a verbatim quote from a primary source or from trade press on two separate domains, search excerpts not counted, in place of a fixed line. Its identity chip prints the number of unconfirmed identity matches among its findings and is left out when there is none, and it no longer prints "Identity confirmed". The coverage card of the report shows the share of dated sources without counts. On the screening endpoints of `/v1` the `coverage_note` states its search, page and date caveats as plain sentences without counts.
- 30 September. `GET /v1/deals` orders by one column when `sort` names it. `score`, `value`, `last_activity`, `stage`, `name` and `first_seen` each order the whole filtered book by that column alone, the ranking window playing no part, and the new `direction` takes `desc` or `asc`. Rows without a value come last in both directions. The ranking window order is now called `priority` and stays the default, so a request without `sort` is answered as before, while a request that named `score`, `value`, `last_activity` or `first_seen` gets that column's order instead of the window first order. An unknown `sort` or `direction` answers 422. The MCP tool `deal_book` takes the same `sort` and `direction`. See the [API reference](/v1).
- 30 September. Every deal row on `GET /v1/deals` and `GET /v1/deals/{deal_id}` carries `latest_update`, the newest event a source dated and confirmed on the deal's timeline with a short label, and `has_unseen_update`, whether that event reached the deal since you last opened it in the dashboard (`null` for an API key). `sort=latest_update` orders the book by that event alone, newest first, and the MCP tool `deal_book` carries the same fields and sort. See the [API reference](/v1).
- 30 September. On `/v1/deals` and the MCP tool `deal_book`, every deal row carries its Value, in `value_status`, `value_text`, `value_note`, `value_finding_id` and `value_basis`. A figure a verified quote states reads `confirmed` with the limit word the quotes set before it, such as up to $375 million. A figure no verified quote confirms reads `reported`, and `value_note` holds the sentence to print beside it, the same sentence the deal summary prints. `value_text` is in the currency the source reported, and the deal PDF, the committee pack, the briefing decks and the Deal Book workbook print the same Value, where they printed a dollar figure converted at a fixed rate before. `value_basis` says whether the Value is the financing amount the newest grounded finding states beside a debt or cover noun, a contract value standing in, or a reported figure no quote classifies, never the largest figure, a project cost or a programme size. See the [API reference](/v1).
- 30 September. On `/v1/deals` and the MCP tool `deal_book`, `last_event_at` is the earliest publication date among the fetched pages of the deal's newest reported event where no source stated an event date, a fetched page of a primary source dating that event ahead of the other pages, and a search snippet never dates a row. Neither `event_date` nor `last_event_at` is ever later than the current day in UTC. The stale window is 12 months by default, down from 18. The evidence reason of a row that no two independent sources corroborate names the number of distinct pages that cite it, such as Cited by 3 pages, no independent second witness. See the [API reference](/v1).
- 26 September. Screening verdict text reads as whole sentences on every surface. On `GET /v1/screenings/{screening_id}` each assessment's `rationale` is labelled machine drafted and cites its findings only as `[f<id>]` tokens naming its own `finding_ids` (a cited finding that verification capped as unverified has no entry in `findings`), and the new `rationale_withheld` counts the sentences held back because they could not be shown with their citations. The screening report PDF labels its summary as machine drafted. While a completed screening of a company waits on an analyst, the `coverage_line` of its risk assessment says "a completed screening awaits analyst confirmation" instead of saying that no analyst confirmed screening is on record; the adverse media row's `gap_reason` stays `screening_stale`. The MCP tools `screening_lookup` and `entity_risk_profile` forward the same fields. See the [API reference](/v1).

A line of the deal's summary no longer says an amount is not confirmed by a source when a verified quote states it. See the [API reference](/v1).

The rows carry the weight the corporate models leave, so the denominators do not move.
