# Main prompts log (ITAC 2026 final, 4 Nov 2026)

This log was kept while we worked, not written afterwards. Times are Asia/Jerusalem ("~" means ±3 min). Prompts keep the user's real wording, typos included. A row marked "(summary)" paraphrases a prompt whose exact wording was not saved. No credential values appear here.

The judges say they are not looking at who produced the most code with AI. They look at engineering judgment, the quality of the solution and the ability to explain decisions. So each row also records **what the human decided**, and the reasons are in [AI_USAGE.md § Key decisions](AI_USAGE.md#key-decisions-and-rationale).
